

The **normal** is any subgroup which is preserved under conjugation.

The **quotient** is the set of cosets of a subgroup, notated $G/N$. 


The normal gives nescesaary conditions for the quotient to be a subgroup

> # Normal Subgroups and Quotient Groups — Exercises
> 
> - 1. Let $G$ be an abelian group and $H \leq G$. Prove that $H \trianglelefteq G$.
> 
> - 2. Let $G = \mathbb{Z}$ and $H = 5\mathbb{Z}$. Show that $H \trianglelefteq G$ and describe the quotient group $\mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}$.
> 
> - 3. Prove that $\{e\}$ and $G$ are normal subgroups of every group $G$.
> 
> - 4. Let $H \leq G$. Prove that $H \trianglelefteq G$ if and only if $gH = Hg$ for every $g \in G$.
> 
> - 5. Determine all normal subgroups of $S_3$.
> 
> - 6. Let $H = \langle (123) \rangle \leq S_3$. Compute the cosets of $H$ and determine the structure of $S_3/H$.
> 
> - 7. Show that $\langle (12) \rangle$ is not a normal subgroup of $S_3$.
> 
> - 8. Let $G$ be a group and suppose $H \leq G$ has index $2$. Prove that $H \trianglelefteq G$.
> 
> - 9. Let $N \trianglelefteq G$. Prove that multiplication of cosets defined by
>   $(aN)(bN) = abN$
>   is well-defined.
> 
> - 10. Let $G = \mathbb{Z}_{12}$ and $H = \langle 4 \rangle$. Determine $G/H$ explicitly and identify it up to isomorphism.
> 
> - 11. Let $G = S_4$ and let
>   $V_4 = \{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}$.
>   Prove that $V_4 \trianglelefteq S_4$ and determine $S_4/V_4$ up to isomorphism.
> 
> - 12. Let $\varphi:G\to H$ be a group homomorphism. Prove that $\ker(\varphi) \trianglelefteq G$.
> 
> - 13. Prove that every normal subgroup $N \trianglelefteq G$ occurs as the kernel of some group homomorphism.
> 
> - 14. Let $N \trianglelefteq G$ and define the canonical projection
>   $\pi:G\to G/N$ by $\pi(g)=gN$.
>   Determine $\ker(\pi)$ and $\operatorname{im}(\pi)$.
> 
> - 15. Let $\varphi:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}_{8}$ be given by $\varphi(n)=[3n]_8$. Find $\ker(\varphi)$ and use the First Isomorphism Theorem to identify $\mathbb{Z}/\ker(\varphi)$.
> 
> - 16. Suppose $N \trianglelefteq G$ and $H \leq G$. Prove that $HN$ is a subgroup of $G$, and prove that
>   $H/(H\cap N) \cong HN/N$.
> 
> - 17. Let $N \trianglelefteq G$ and let $H$ be a subgroup satisfying $N\leq H\leq G$. Prove that
>   $H/N \trianglelefteq G/N$
>   if and only if
>   $H \trianglelefteq G$.
> 
> - 18. Prove the Correspondence Theorem: if $N\trianglelefteq G$, then the subgroups of $G/N$ are in bijection with the subgroups $H\leq G$ satisfying $N\leq H$. Show that normal subgroups correspond to normal subgroups.
> 
> - 19. Suppose $N,M\trianglelefteq G$ with $N\leq M$. Prove the Third Isomorphism Theorem:
>   $(G/N)/(M/N)\cong G/M$.
> 
> - 20. Let $G$ be a group with normal subgroups $N,M\trianglelefteq G$ such that $N\cap M=\{e\}$ and $NM=G$. Prove that every element of $N$ commutes with every element of $M$, and deduce that
>   $G\cong N\times M$.
