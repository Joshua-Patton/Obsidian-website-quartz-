> [! exercise]-
>
> - Prove that every abelian group is solvable.
> - Compute the derived subgroup $G'=[G,G]$ when $G=C_n$.
> - Compute the derived series of $C_4\times C_6$.
> - Show that $S_3'=A_3$.
> - Compute the complete derived series of $S_3$ and show that $S_3$ is solvable.
> - Compute the derived subgroup of the dihedral group $D_8=\langle r,s\mid r^4=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$.
> - Show directly from the derived series that $D_8$ is solvable.
> - Let $G$ satisfy $G'\subseteq Z(G)$. Prove that $G$ is solvable.
> - Suppose $G'$ is abelian. Show that $G$ is solvable.
> - If $G^{(2)}=\{e\}$, show that $G'$ is abelian.
> - Suppose $N\triangleleft G$, with both $N$ and $G/N$ abelian. Prove that $G$ is solvable.
> - Suppose $N\triangleleft G$, and both $N$ and $G/N$ are solvable. Prove that $G$ is solvable.
> - If $G$ is solvable and $H\leq G$, prove that $H$ is solvable.
> - If $G$ is solvable and $N\triangleleft G$, prove that $G/N$ is solvable.
> - Prove that $G\times H$ is solvable if and only if both $G$ and $H$ are solvable.
> - Show that every group of order $p^2$, where $p$ is prime, is solvable.
> - Show that every finite $p$-group is solvable.
> - Prove that every nilpotent group is solvable.
> - Suppose $G$ has a normal series
>   $1=G_0\triangleleft G_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft G_n=G$
>   where every quotient $G_{i+1}/G_i$ is cyclic. Prove that $G$ is solvable.
> - Show that every finite group having a composition series whose composition factors are cyclic is solvable.
> - Prove that $S_4$ is solvable by finding its derived series.
> - Show that $S_4'=A_4$, and determine $A_4'$.
> - Show that the Klein four group
>   $V_4=\{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}$
>   is normal in $A_4$, and use
>   $1\triangleleft V_4\triangleleft A_4\triangleleft S_4$
>   to prove that $S_4$ is solvable.
> - Determine the derived length of $S_4$.
> - Prove that $A_5$ is not solvable, using the fact that $A_5$ is nonabelian and simple.
> - Let $\phi:G\to H$ be a surjective homomorphism. Prove that
>   $\phi(G')=H'$.
>   Deduce that every homomorphic image of a solvable group is solvable.
> - Prove by induction that for any homomorphism $\phi:G\to H$,
>   $\phi(G^{(n)})\subseteq H^{(n)}$.
>   When does equality hold?
> - Let $G$ and $H$ be solvable of derived lengths $m$ and $n$. Find an upper bound for the derived length of $G\times H$, and then determine its exact derived length.
> - Suppose
>   $1\to N\to G\to Q\to1$
>   is a short exact sequence and $N,Q$ are solvable of derived lengths $m,n$. Prove that $G$ is solvable and show that its derived length is at most $m+n$.
> - Let $f(x)\in\mathbb{Q}[x]$ have splitting field $K$ and suppose
>   $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})\cong S_3$.
>   Show that its Galois group is solvable and explain the connection with solvability of $f(x)$ by radicals.
